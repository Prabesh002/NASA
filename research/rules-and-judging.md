## Team
- 1–6 members (Universal Event: at least 2 for global judging). Everyone must be registered at the same Local Event and not waitlisted.
- Each person can be on one team, and each team works on one challenge. Members can't be added after submission closes.

## SI (AI) policy (Project Submission Guide)
- The FAQ cites a Sept 29, 2026 U.S. Executive Order: "Super Intelligence"/"SI" replaces "AI".
- SI use is permitted "as a tool to accelerate innovation and creativity". **"SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)."**
- "Generated content may not use, contain, or modify any NASA branding or design elements (such as the NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers)."
- We must state where and how SI was used:
  - Images and videos need a visible watermark.
  - Audio needs acknowledgment in descriptive text and metadata.
  - Code and data need acknowledgment in descriptive text and metadata.
- The SI question on the form is data collection only and not shown to judges. Judges do see the in-project SI indicators (watermarks, text, metadata) and "evaluate originality, intent, and execution" based on them.

## Submission form (required fields)
1. Project name
2. High-level summary: what we built, how it addresses the challenge, why it matters
3. **Demo link**: video ≤ 30 s **or** slides ≤ 7 (including title and intro slides). Publicly accessible. English, or English subtitles for a video. Exceeding the limits means **ineligible for Global Judging**.
4. Final project link: public, with no login, password or registration
5. Project details: what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools and languages used
6. **NASA data and resources used.** At least one is required for Global Award eligibility.
7. Space Agency Partner data (encouraged)
8. All references: data, code, text and images, including open-source ones
9. SI usage question

Other notes:
- The project page accepts images (JPG, PNG or GIF, up to 10 MB). Videos and documents have to be linked.
- The guide suggests requesting a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification for a new website so judges' networks don't block it. Optional, but relevant for `nasa.nesomotions.com`.
- We can resubmit until the deadline. The last saved submission is the one judged.

Suggested 30-second video structure (from the guide):
- 0:00 who we are
- 0:02 which challenge, and why
- 0:05 project name and tagline
- 0:10 how it works, and how NASA and partner data make it possible
- 0:20–0:30 the future impact

## Judging criteria (each scored 1–5, at every level)
- **Impact**: size of the problem addressed and how many it helps or inspires
- **Creativity**: originality; a novel concept or a meaningful improvement on existing ones
- **Validity**: grounded in scientific principles, feasible, usable, user-friendly
- **Relevance**: fully addresses the chosen challenge, and **NASA data is a core component, not a superficial addition**
- **Presentation**: clarity and storytelling, judged on the demo and the full project
